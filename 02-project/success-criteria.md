# Success Criteria

The project will be evaluated across five dimensions.

| Dimension | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|
| Systems thinking | Can explain how individual controls affect the wider system |
| Coordination | Can identify dependencies, owners and handoffs |
| GRC | Can translate requirements into practical governance activities |
| Project management | Can sequence work, manage risks and escalate blockers |
| Assurance | Can distinguish documentation, implementation, evidence and effectiveness |

## Final Capability Test

At v1.0, the learner should be able to receive a messy cross-functional GRC situation and answer:

1. What is the desired outcome?
2. What requirement or risk drives it?
3. Who owns each part?
4. What dependencies exist?
5. What can block delivery?
6. What evidence should exist?
7. How will effectiveness be tested?
8. What needs escalation?
9. What decision belongs to management?
10. How will the result be communicated?

The goal is not to memorize the analogy.

The goal is to develop the habit of seeing the whole system.
